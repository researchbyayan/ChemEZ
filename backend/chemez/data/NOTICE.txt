c13_shifts.json.gz is a machine-readable derived database of aggregated carbon
environments and observed shifts, based on the 2024-06-06 source data. It retains
the nmrshiftdb2 Database License; it is not relicensed under ChemEZ's MIT license.
The transformation algorithm is scripts/build-c13-table.py in the ChemEZ source
release. Predictions using aggregate records are not measurements of a sample.
